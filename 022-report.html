Does the model beat a simple baseline?

The model's holdout MAE is 16.75, compared with 19.65 for the baseline. This is 14.8% lower error on the same 3,231 observations.

MAE is average absolute prediction error; lower is better. Model choices are fixed for this demonstration, with a chronological 2023 development check and a separate 2024 evaluation. These historical results are not a guarantee of future performance.

Stability matters

Both models have higher MAE than their baselines in the 2023 validation check. The 2024 improvement does not establish consistent superiority across years. For energy, the baseline also has lower 2024 RMSLE: the ranking depends on the error measure. Large reported outliers remain in the data and can dominate RMSE; no post-holdout outlier removal was used.

Interpretation limits

The eligible cohort requires compliant, active records in both years and usable prior-year floor area and age. Results therefore describe continuing eligible reporters, not all buildings. Property IDs recur across time by design. EUI is weather normalized but this is an observational prediction exercise, not an estimate of retrofit savings. Exclude 2025 pending source metadata review. No uncertainty intervals or deployment claims.

Reproduce and inspect
