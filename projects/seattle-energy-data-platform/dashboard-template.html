Definitions & provenance

EUI is site energy use per square foot, weather normalized, in kBtu/ft². The median includes only records marked Compliant, not demolished, with positive, nonmissing EUI. Each property contributes one value; medians are recomputed from records after filtering. Compliance rate uses every record in the selected cohort as its denominator.

The programme covers qualifying buildings, generally over 20,000 square feet. These are reported values, subject to revision, not a census of every Seattle building. Comparisons do not establish causes or savings. No missing values are replaced with zero.
