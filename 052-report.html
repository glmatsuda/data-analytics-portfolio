Does the model beat a simple baseline?

The model's holdout MAE is 501.69, compared with 890.95 for the baseline. This is 43.7% lower error on the same 365 observations.

MAE is average absolute prediction error; lower is better. Model choices are fixed for this demonstration, with a chronological 2023 development check and a separate 2024 evaluation. These historical results are not a guarantee of future performance.

Interpretation limits

The source currently describes combined bicycle and scooter counts; column headers still say Cyclist. Counts are detections, not unique people. Sidewalk columns retain the source labels without inferring travel direction. Local floating timestamps lack an offset; days with other than 24 unique valid hours, including daylight-saving ambiguities, are excluded. No weather, holidays or causal interventions are modelled. This is a retrospective calendar-only prediction exercise, not a production forecast.
