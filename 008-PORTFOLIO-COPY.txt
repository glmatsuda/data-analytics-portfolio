TITLE
RStudio: Building Energy Prediction with Temporal Validation

DESCRIPTION
Developed an interpretable R regression study using prior-year building attributes to predict next-year weather-normalized EUI. Included chronological validation, a prior-year baseline, reproducible outputs and a model card. On 3,231 eligible 2024 records, MAE was 16.75 versus 19.65 for baseline, while other years and metrics exposed limitations. Independent, AI-assisted public-data portfolio demonstration; no client or city affiliation.

SUGGESTED SKILLS
R, data science, statistical modelling, data quality, reproducible research
